# Neraca design direction

Status: implementation direction inferred from the current Neraca product brief
and UI. This is a working brief, not a user-authored brand system. Revise it
when the product owner supplies a different identity.

## Identity

Neraca is a quiet personal-finance workspace for one person. It should feel
clear, grounded, private, and useful during routine monthly review. It is not a
trading terminal, a fintech marketing page, or a gamified savings app.

## Personality

- Calm and precise rather than loud.
- Direct Indonesian copy rather than promotional language.
- Numbers are evidence, not decoration.
- Empty data is honest and actionable.

## Visual direction

- Use deep green as the primary action and identity role.
- Use warm paper and mint surfaces to separate hierarchy without carding every row.
- Use coral only for negative or destructive financial meaning.
- Use a restrained lavender or amber role only when it communicates a distinct state.
- Keep the existing desktop-first workspace structure, but make compact layouts intentional.

## Interaction direction

- The primary action is recording a transaction.
- Navigation changes destination; buttons change data in the current context.
- Use dialogs for focused data entry and snackbars for brief save/restore feedback.
- Destructive actions need explicit confirmation and recovery where possible.
- Motion is limited to state change and spatial feedback.

## Quality dials

- ENERGY: 2. Calm, with one or two deliberate moments of emphasis.
- RHYTHM: 3. Vary dashboard density between summary, flow, and detail regions.
- MOTION: 1. Keep financial reading surfaces stable; animate only feedback.
